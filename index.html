Live to Serve. Love to Serve.

UBC Circle K International (CKI) is a student-led service club sponsored by the Kiwanis Club of Vancouver. We bring together UBC students who care about giving back, building leadership skills, and making a positive impact in both local and global communities.

See upcoming events Learn about CKI

What we do

Service

Volunteer with local organizations and take on projects that help people in our community and beyond.

Leadership

Plan events, run committees and build skills you can use well past graduation.

Friendship

Meet people who care about the same things you do, at socials, service days and everything in between.

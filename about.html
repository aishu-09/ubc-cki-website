About UBC Circle K International

A student-led service club for people who want to give back.

Who we are

UBC Circle K International (CKI) is a student-led service club sponsored by the Kiwanis Club of Vancouver. We bring together UBC students who care about giving back, building leadership skills, and making a positive impact in both local and global communities.

Members can take part in monthly volunteer opportunities, campus events, and international conferences while gaining hands-on experience in leadership, advocacy, and teamwork. We aim to create a welcoming space for students to get involved and learn.

If you're looking for a way to give back and meet new people without overcommitting as a student, UBC CKI is a great place to start!

What members do

  • Volunteer monthly. Join service projects with community partners in Vancouver.
  • Come to campus events. Socials, service days and club activities at UBC.
  • Attend international conferences. Meet Circle K members from other schools and countries.
  • Build real skills. Get hands-on experience in leadership, advocacy and teamwork.

Causes we care about

Childhood development

Supporting kids and the programs that help them grow.

Environmental justice

Working toward a healthier environment for everyone.

Mental health

Building a campus and community where people support each other.

Food and housing insecurity

Helping neighbours who are struggling to meet basic needs.

Part of something bigger

Circle K International is the premier collegiate and university community service, leadership development and friendship organization in the world. Our club is sponsored by the Kiwanis Club of Vancouver and belongs to the larger Kiwanis family, which gives members a network that reaches well beyond campus.
